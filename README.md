What’s New

We’ve improved the app to make your listening experience smoother and more personal.

Music Playlist Updates

Updated and cleaned up playlists to match different moods.

Energy ⚡ now features Alternative Metalcore and Deathcore.

Focus 🎯 includes Ashh, Classical Focus, and Instrumental Study.

Chill 🛋️ brings K-Loviee, Larkspur, and Ambient Workspace.

All playlists are now connected to valid YouTube links.

New Features

Activity Filter. Easily switch between All, Focus, Energy, and Chill.

Favorites. Tap the star icon to save your favorite playlists.

Favorites are stored locally, so they stay saved even after refresh.

Quick access button in the header to view favorites only.

Playback History. View the last 10 tracks you played with time stamps.

Design Improvements

Modern look with a clean purple and orange theme.

Fully responsive layout for mobile and desktop.

Smooth animations on buttons, playlist cards, and color transitions.
